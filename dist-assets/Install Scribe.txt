Installing Scribe
=================

1. Drag Scribe onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Eject the "Scribe" disk (the eject button next to it in Finder's sidebar).
3. Open Applications and double-click Scribe.

The first time, macOS says it cannot verify Scribe and offers only "Done"
or "Move to Trash". Click Done, then:

4. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
5. Scroll down to the message about Scribe and click "Open Anyway".
6. Enter your password if asked, then click "Open Anyway" once more.

You only do this once. Later updates open normally.

Scribe lives in the menu bar (top right of the screen), not the Dock.

Permissions
-----------
When you first record, Scribe needs two switches turned on in
System Settings > Privacy & Security:

  - Screen & System Audio Recording  (to take the screenshots)
  - Accessibility                    (to name what you clicked)

If Scribe is not listed, click +, pick Scribe from Applications, turn it on,
then quit Scribe from its menu and open it again. A permission never applies
to the copy that was already running.

Updating
--------
Download the new DMG and drag Scribe to Applications again, choosing Replace.
Your guides and permissions carry over.
